This Privacy Policy describes how Sweepio ("we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application. Sweepio is a file management and storage analysis tool that helps you understand storage usage, manage files, find duplicates and large files, and clean up unnecessary data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.
1

Information We Collect and Use

Sweepio is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our own servers.

  • We do not collect personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • We do not collect persistent hardware device identifiers such as IMEI directly
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not collect browsing history or core usage analytics by ourselves
  • We do not share any data with third parties beyond what is described in Section 4

All file scanning, storage analysis, and management operations are performed entirely on your device. No file or user personal data ever leaves your device through our own services.

Note on Advertising: Our advertising partner Google AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (including Android Advertising ID / GAID and other temporary device IDs), device model, OS version to serve ads, measure ad performance and prevent ad fraud. These identifiers are shared with authorized ad platforms for advertising attribution, analytics and fraud prevention purposes via encrypted transmission. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your Android system settings at any time, and this choice will not affect core basic functions of the app.

2

Permissions We Request

Sweepio requires certain permissions to perform its file management and storage analysis functions. Each permission is used solely for its stated purpose:

  • Storage / Manage External Storage: Required to scan device storage for file categorization, storage analysis, duplicate detection, large file identification, and file management. This permission allows the app to read file metadata, analyze storage usage, and manage selected files.
  • Package Usage Stats (Usage Access): Required to calculate app cache sizes and provide accurate storage usage information for each installed application.
  • Query All Packages: Required for the following core features: (1) scanning uninstall residuals left by removed apps, (2) storage analysis by application, (3) app cache cleaning, (4) running app detection to protect active apps from being affected, and (5) archived app detection to identify retained data. This permission allows the app to identify which apps are installed and detect files left behind after uninstallation.
  • Accessibility Service: Used exclusively to automate clicking the "Clear Cache" button in system settings for apps whose cache cannot be cleared programmatically. The accessibility service does not collect, read, store, or transmit any personal data. It only interacts with the system Settings UI to perform cache clearing. It does not access your screen content, personal information, messages, or any other sensitive data. You can disable this service at any time in Settings > Accessibility.
  • Request Delete Packages: Required to support app uninstallation functionality.
  • Request Install Packages: Required to install APK files opened from within the app.
  • Post Notifications: Required to send notifications about uninstall residual alerts and file management results.
3

Data Storage and Security

All scan results and temporary data are stored in your device's memory (RAM) only. When you close the app or restart your device, this temporary data is automatically cleared. No persistent database of your files or personal information is created.

Deleted files are moved to an in-app recycle bin (stored locally on your device) with a configurable auto-deletion period, giving you time to recover accidentally deleted items. Files in the recycle bin are permanently deleted after the configured period.

When you uninstall Sweepio, all app data including the recycle bin is permanently removed from your device.

4

Third-Party Services

Sweepio uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (including Android Advertising ID / GAID), device model and OS version for personalized ad delivery, advertising attribution, ad performance analytics and fraud prevention. These identifiers are transmitted securely to authorized ad partners for these legitimate purposes and retained per platform rules and applicable regulations. AdMob's data practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization) or reset your advertising ID anytime.
  • Google Play In-App Update: We use Google Play Core's In-App Update API to check for and prompt app updates. This service communicates with Google Play to determine if an update is available. See Google's Privacy Policy.

We do not use any analytics services, crash reporting services, or social sharing features operated directly by us.

5

Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request information about the data we process about you. Since we do not collect personal data directly, there is typically no data to access beyond what is stored locally on your device and data processed by third-party ad providers.
  • Right to Deletion: You can delete all local app data at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or uninstalling the app. For ad identifier data managed by Google, please adjust your system ad settings accordingly.
  • Right to Opt Out of Personalized Ads: You can opt out of personalized advertising by Google AdMob through your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization) or by resetting your advertising ID.
  • Right to Revoke Permissions: You can revoke any permission granted to the app at any time through your device settings (Settings > Apps > Sweepio > Permissions).

To exercise any of these rights, you do not need to contact us — all controls are available directly on your device. If you have questions, you may contact us at the email address in Section 8.

6

Children's Privacy

Sweepio does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we do not collect any personal data from any users regardless of age, our app is safe for users of all ages. We do not target children specifically, and we do not offer any child-directed content or services.

Our app is not designated as a "Designed for Families" app on Google Play. Parents or guardians may wish to supervise younger children when using the app, as it includes the ability to manage and delete files.
